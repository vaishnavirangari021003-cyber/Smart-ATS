Recruitment Pipeline Transition Rules

HireFlow enforces strict forward workflow integrity and auditability:

  • Applied → Screening or Rejected
  • Screening → Interview, Rejected, or Applied (revert)
  • Interview → Selected, Rejected, or Screening (revert)
  • Selected → Offered, Rejected, or Interview (revert)
  • Offered → Rejected (only if offer declined or withdrawn)
Validation Guards:
  • Moving to Interview requires at least one scheduled interview session.
  • Moving to Offered requires a draft or issued offer letter.
  • Rejected and Withdrawn stages are terminal.
