Measure funnel conversion, time-to-hire, offer acceptance velocity, and pipeline bottlenecks.

ℹ️ Official Metric Definitions & Calculation Rules (Click to expand)
  • Stage conversion: Candidates who entered stage N / candidates who entered stage N-1, within the selected cohort and date window.
  • Time-to-hire: Calendar days from appliedAt to offer acceptance, computed strictly for accepted offers. Incomplete or missing date records are excluded (0 excluded).
  • Offer acceptance rate: Accepted offers / total offers with a candidate response (Accepted + Declined).
  • Notification delivery success: Successfully sent notifications / total attempts (Simulated).
